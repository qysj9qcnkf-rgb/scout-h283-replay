1. The TV news heard as the shopper

From [7:16] to [8:50] a CBC news report on her TV went into the call as her words. It cut Mia off and kept her silent for a minute and a half. The shopper said "Hello?" and "I lost her."

Fix: on store calls, speech-to-text now labels who is speaking. Mia learns the shopper's voice from her answers. Another voice that keeps talking (14 words in 30 seconds) counts as background: its words are taken out before Mia hears them, so they don't count toward the code, don't become a shopper turn and can't cut Mia off. While the TV is on, Mia's replies don't wait for the line to go quiet and can't be cut off, and the shopper's turn ends 1.5 seconds after her own words stop, even with the TV still talking. This is mechanical only: nothing Mia says or asks is added or changed.

Replay: the call's recording (with Mia's own lines silenced, as the live agent hears it) run through the same speech-to-text and voice detector, then through this branch's guard.

2. "It's on your screen now" when it wasn't

At [9:44] Mia said the $88 Globemaster was on the shopper's screen. The shopper: "I can't even see it. I don't know where to go to find that."

Why: Replogle's store theme doesn't load inside the call screen, so every product Mia "showed" turned into our stand-in page ("This page lives on the store's own site"). Nothing told Mia.

Fix: the page in the call screen now reports whether it really showed. Mia waits for that report (up to 6 seconds) before she says a product is on screen. If it didn't show (the stand-in page, an error, or nothing in time), she gives the search words straight away.

3. Mia cut off by one short sound (a rule check, not an audio replay)

At [3:17] Mia stopped at "Good to know. When". Her log heard one word, "Hello?", which isn't in the recording (only Mia's voice is there). A stray sound turned into one word and stopped her.

Fix: on store calls the shopper must say two words to cut Mia off (LiveKit's own min_interruption_words rule). The "Hello?" isn't in the recording, so this is checked with LiveKit's own rule on Mia's logged word, not on audio. The trade-off: a single "Stop" or "Wait" no longer cuts Mia off either.
