1. The TV news heard as the shopper

From [7:16] to [8:50] a CBC news report on her TV went into the call as her words, cut Mia off and kept her silent for a minute and a half. The shopper said "Hello?" and "I lost her."

Fix: on store calls, speech-to-text now labels who is speaking. Mia learns the shopper's voice from her answers. Another voice that keeps talking (14 words in 30 seconds) is treated as background: its words are taken out before Mia hears them, so they don't count toward the code, don't become a shopper turn and can't cut Mia off. While it lasts, Mia's replies don't wait for the line to go quiet and can't be cut, and she says once that she hears a TV.

Replay: the call's recording (Mia's own lines silenced, as the live agent hears it) through the same speech-to-text, then this branch's guard.

2. "It's on your screen now" when it wasn't

At [9:44] Mia said the $88 Globemaster was on screen; the shopper: "I can't even see it. I don't know where to go to find that."

Why: Replogle's store theme doesn't load inside the call screen, so every product Mia "showed" became our stand-in page ("This page lives on the store's own site"). Mia was never told.

Fix: the page in the call screen now reports whether it really showed. Mia waits for that (up to 6 seconds) before she says a product is on screen. If it didn't show (the stand-in page, an error, nothing in time), she gives the search words straight away.

3. Mia cut off by one short sound

At [3:17] Mia stopped at "Good to know. When". Her log heard one word, "Hello?", which isn't in the recording (only Mia's voice is there), so a stray sound turned into one word and stopped her.

Fix: on store calls the shopper must say two words to cut Mia off (LiveKit's own min_interruption_words rule).
